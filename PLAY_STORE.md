**App name:** Medi Info  (max 30)
**Short description (80):** Find verified hospitals & doctors in Bengaluru and book appointments fast.
**Category:** Medical  ·  **Tags:** Health, Appointments  ·  **Content rating:** complete the questionnaire (expect "Everyone")

**Full description:**
Medi Info helps you choose the right hospital and book the right doctor, without the phone calls.

• Discover verified hospitals and clinics near you, on a map or in a list
• Filter by department, rating, distance and consultation type
• See doctors, qualifications, experience and fees
• Book a time slot in under a minute, with live availability
• Pay online securely with UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets (powered by Cashfree) where the hospital offers it
• Reschedule or cancel from the app; eligible online payments are refunded
• Get directions to the hospital in one tap
• Save favourite hospitals and view your booking history

Medi Info is an appointment platform. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency call 112.
Launching in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
