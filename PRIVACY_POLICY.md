# Medi Info Privacy Policy
*Last updated: October 2026. DRAFT: have a lawyer review for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 before publishing. Replace [bracketed] items.*

**Who we are.** Medi Info ([legal entity name, address]) operates the Medi Info app and website. Contact / grievance officer: [name], support@mediinfo.in.

**What we collect.** Mobile number and name (account); patient name, age, gender, phone and reason for visit (when you book); approximate or precise location only when you choose "near me" or save a hospital location; booking and payment status (order ID, amount, status). **We never receive or store your card, UPI or bank details. Cashfree processes them.**

**Why.** To create your account via OTP, book and manage appointments, share booking details with the hospital you choose, send booking notifications, process payments and refunds, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

**Who sees it.** The hospital and doctor you book with (your booking details and phone number); Cashfree Payments (payment processing); our hosting and SMS providers ([names]) as processors. We do not sell personal data and show no advertising.

**Security.** HTTPS, hashed one-time codes, role-based access, audit logs, rate-limiting and database backups.

**Retention.** Account data until you delete your account. Appointment and payment records may be retained in anonymised or legally required form (e.g. tax/accounting) for [8] years.

**Your rights.** Access, correct or erase your data and withdraw consent: delete your account in-app (Account → Delete my account) or write to support@mediinfo.in. Web deletion request page: [URL, required by Google Play].

**Children.** Medi Info is for adults. Parents/guardians may book on behalf of a child.

**Medical disclaimer.** Medi Info does not provide medical advice. In an emergency call 112.
